How we source Credible, and copyright-clean

  • Primary sources first: SEBI press releases, AMFI data, and the fund houses' own scheme documents whenever possible.
  • Reputable press second: established financial publications — always linked, never copied. Every story is summarized in our own words.
  • Facts are free: dates, minimum investments, and NAVs can't be copyrighted — but sentences can be, so we never lift them.
  • Our charts are ours: any data visualization is computed from public NAV histories, not screenshotted from anyone.
