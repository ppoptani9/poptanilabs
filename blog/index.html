How we source Credible, and copyright-clean

  • Primary sources first: company announcements, SEBI releases, and AMFI data on news days — and the thinkers' own books, shows, and podcasts on Big Thinkers Fridays.
  • Reputable press second: established publications — always linked, never copied. Every story is summarized in our own words.
  • Facts are free: dates, figures, and fund details can't be copyrighted — but sentences can be, so we never lift them. Every anecdote is a real, sourced event; if it can't be verified, it doesn't ship.
  • Pictures are licensed: every image is freely licensed (Wikimedia Commons) and credited beneath it — never a copyrighted still, never a hotlink.
