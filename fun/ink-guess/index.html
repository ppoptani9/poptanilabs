How it works

Private by design

The sketch AI runs entirely in your browser — your drawings never leave this device, are never uploaded, and never seen by anyone. There is no account and no server watching you draw.

Trained from scratch, on open data

The sketch AI behind Ink Guess was trained by Poptani Labs on 132,000 hand drawings from Google's Quick, Draw! Dataset, which Google shares under CC BY 4.0. The model weights, the game code, and the interface are our own original work.
