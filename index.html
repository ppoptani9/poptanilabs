About the lab Why these tools exist

Poptani Labs started with a simple frustration: good financial tools for Indian investors are either locked behind sign-ups or buried in spreadsheets. So I build the tools I wish existed — free, private, and honest about their math. The SWP Simulator was the first experiment; it began life as a Python project and grew into this site, and the SIP Calculator and Fund X-Ray joined it. The lab keeps growing from here.

Everything here is a work in progress and open to feedback. Found a bug, want a feature, or have an experiment in mind? Say hello — every suggestion is read by a human.

A note: tools on this site are educational aids, not financial advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks — read all scheme documents carefully.
