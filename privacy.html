Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing about you. This calculator runs entirely in your browser. Your fund selections, amounts and dates are never sent to us, stored on a server, or tied to an identity. There are no accounts and no analytics that track your inputs.

What is stored on your device

  • Your theme preference (dark/light) in localStorage.
  • A cached copy of the mutual-fund scheme list in localStorage, so the list loads faster on repeat visits. You can clear it any time via your browser's site-data settings.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Learn more in Google's advertising & privacy documentation.

Third-party data

NAV and scheme data are fetched from the public API at mfapi.in; chart rendering uses the Chart.js library from a CDN. Those services receive only the technical requests needed to deliver data and code — never your calculator inputs.
