FAQs

What returns did a SIP in HDFC Flexi Cap Fund actually give?

A ₹10,000 monthly SIP in HDFC Flexi Cap Fund over the last 10 years would have grown to ₹28.09L on an investment of ₹12.00L — an XIRR of 16.28%. For instance, a 1-year SIP ending 2026-10-01 would currently show a paper loss of ₹3.98K — short-term volatility is normal in equity funds.

How is SIP XIRR different from the fund's advertised returns?

Advertised returns are point-to-point (lump sum at two dates). SIP XIRR accounts for money going in every month at different NAVs — it's the return your staggered investment actually earned, which is usually lower than the headline number in rising markets and higher in falling ones.

What is the minimum SIP amount?

Most Indian equity funds accept ₹500–₹1,000 per month. Confirm the exact minimum in the scheme's SID/KIM before starting.

Can a SIP in HDFC Flexi Cap Fund lose money?

Yes. A SIP doesn't remove market risk — it only averages your purchase price. Over short periods the value can sit below what you invested, as the 1-year row above sometimes shows.

Where do these numbers come from?
