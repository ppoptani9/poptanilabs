FAQs

How did a SWP from Aditya Birla Sun Life Flexi Cap Fund perform?

Investing ₹10 lakh five years ago and withdrawing ₹6,000/month would have paid out ₹3.60L while leaving ₹11.88L still invested — an XIRR of 10.56%. The corpus was never depleted.

Is 7.2% annual withdrawal sustainable?

₹6,000/month on ₹10 lakh is 7.2% in year one. Whether a withdrawal rate is sustainable depends on the fund's long-run returns minus that rate — as a rule of thumb, staying at or under ~3% a year keeps a corpus growing in most markets. Test your own rate in the simulator.

What happens if a withdrawal is bigger than the balance?

The plan pays out whatever remains and ends — our simulator models this exactly, redeeming the leftover units instead of failing.

Are SWP withdrawals taxed?

Each withdrawal is a redemption and taxed as capital gains — equity funds held over 12 months qualify for long-term treatment (12.5% above the ₹1.25L annual exemption, per current rules). This illustration is pre-tax.

Where do these numbers come from?

Daily NAV history published under AMFI, fetched via mfapi.in, replayed with the same engine as our SWP simulator. A historical illustration, not a prediction.
